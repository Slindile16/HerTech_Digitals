A NOTE FROM YOUR DEVELOPER

Wondering who’s behind
HerTech_Digital?

My name is Slindile Mthimunye. I’m 20 years old and based in Daveyton, Etwatwa. I’m a software engineering student who’s curious about how technology works and excited by what it can make possible.

I’m building my skills in software development and web design, and I enjoy turning ideas into useful, welcoming online experiences. I work with HTML, CSS, Python, Java and SQL. Through my studies and personal projects, I’m learning to solve problems, think through the details and keep improving my work.

I created HerTech_Digital to bring my interest in technology and web development into one place. I want to help people and small businesses build a confident online presence with websites that are clear, thoughtful and shaped around what they do.

No confusing jargon or one-size-fits-all templates—just a collaborative process, thoughtful details and a digital home made around you.

When we work together, I’ll take time to understand your idea and what you want your website to do. I value clear communication, useful design and a collaborative process—so the finished site feels like yours and helps you take your next step online.
